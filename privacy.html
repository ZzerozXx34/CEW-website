Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Notice explains how C.E.W. handles personal information submitted through this website and related public-facing interactions.

Privacy contact: comfort.encode.workplace@gmail.com. The website files supplied for this review did not contain a separately verified Data Protection Officer identity, so none is represented here.

1. Information we collect

  • Consultation and application information: name, work email, business objective/message, selected pod configuration, and information you choose to include in a request.
  • Review information: name/title, rating, service/pod tier, review text, and publication authorization.
  • Technical information: information that may be processed automatically by hosting, security, browser, font, or third-party service infrastructure when you visit or interact with the site.

Do not submit passwords, payment-card information, government identifiers, health information, or other sensitive information through the public website forms unless C.E.W. has specifically instructed you to use a secure channel.

2. Why we process information

3. Legal basis and transparency

Depending on the situation, processing may be necessary to respond to a request, perform or prepare for a contract, comply with a legal obligation, protect legitimate interests, or rely on consent where consent is the appropriate basis. Where consent is used, it may be withdrawn subject to lawful exceptions and consequences.

Under applicable Philippine data-protection rules, data subjects have rights that can include being informed, access, correction, objection, erasure or blocking where warranted, and data portability. These rights are subject to the conditions and exceptions provided by applicable law.

4. Service providers

The current website uses or may connect to service providers including:

  • Formspree: used to transmit website inquiry and review-moderation submissions to C.E.W.'s designated email workflow.
  • Supabase: used to retrieve published reviews from the C.E.W. review database. The public site is configured to read only reviews marked as approved.
  • Google Fonts: used to load website typography.
  • Vercel or the applicable hosting provider: used to host and deliver the website.

These providers may process technical or submitted information according to their own services and policies. C.E.W. should review and maintain the applicable provider agreements and privacy settings as the service stack changes.

5. Reviews

Review submissions are not published automatically. They are sent for moderation. Published reviews may remain visible until they are removed, become no longer relevant, or a legitimate removal request is accepted. C.E.W. may redact personal or confidential information before publication.

6. Retention

Website inquiry and consultation information is intended to be retained for approximately 24 months after the request is resolved, unless a longer period is required by law, needed to establish or defend a legal claim, or necessary for an ongoing client relationship. Client-service records may be retained according to the client agreement and applicable law.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the information and systems involved. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. If you believe you have discovered a security issue, contact us privately rather than publicly posting credentials, personal data, or exploit details.

8. Your requests

To ask about access, correction, objection, deletion/blocking where applicable, portability, or another privacy matter, email comfort.encode.workplace@gmail.com with enough information for us to understand and verify the request. We may need to verify identity or authority before acting on a request.

9. Children

The website is intended for business users and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly request children's personal information through the site's business forms.

10. Changes

We may update this notice when our processing activities, service providers, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted here with the update date.

11. Philippine privacy framework

Where applicable, C.E.W. seeks to operate in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), its implementing rules, and relevant National Privacy Commission issuances. This notice is intended to describe the site's current processing practices and does not replace a contract or a data-processing agreement where one is required.
