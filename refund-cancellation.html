Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy explains the default website-facing approach to cancellations and refunds. The signed client agreement or order document controls where it contains different, specific terms.

Before paid work begins: published plan prices are estimates of the selected configuration. Final scope, start date, billing cycle, third-party charges, and cancellation terms are confirmed in writing.

1. Website inquiries

Submitting a consultation request, pod application, or review does not create a paid contract. No service fee is charged merely because a form is submitted.

2. Monthly services

Unless a signed agreement states otherwise, monthly service fees are billed according to the agreed billing schedule, generally in advance for the applicable service period. A cancellation normally takes effect at the end of the already-paid service period unless the agreement provides an earlier termination right.

3. Refunds

Unless required by applicable law or expressly provided in the signed agreement, fees for a service period that has already started are not automatically refundable merely because the client stops using the service, changes priorities, or has unused capacity.

Where C.E.W. agrees in writing to a refund, the amount and timing will be confirmed in that written agreement. Third-party charges, advertising spend, software subscriptions, platform fees, domains, hosting, and similar external costs may be non-refundable according to the relevant provider's terms.

4. Service changes and pod-size changes

Requests to increase, decrease, pause, or otherwise change a team configuration are subject to availability, scope, notice requirements, and the applicable client agreement. The website's five-day notice statement is a planning guideline, not a promise that every requested change can be implemented in exactly five days.

5. Disputes about billing

If you believe a charge is incorrect, contact comfort.encode.workplace@gmail.com promptly with the relevant invoice or transaction information. We will review the issue and communicate the applicable resolution under the client agreement and applicable law.

6. Mandatory rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove a refund, cancellation, warranty, or other consumer or contractual right that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.
